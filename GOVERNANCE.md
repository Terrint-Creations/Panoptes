# Governance

## Who runs Panoptes

Panoptes was started by Terrint Creations and is maintained in the open on GitHub. The maintainers
review contributions, decide what goes into the main project and publish releases.

## Contributors

Anyone can contribute. You don't need to ask first: open an issue or a pull request. Contributions
are accepted or declined on their merits and on whether they fit the direction of the main project.

## Project members

People who contribute regularly, or who want to take on a bigger role (maintaining a group of
sources, reviewing pull requests, triaging issues, working on the website or documentation), can
ask to become members of the Panoptes project using the form at
[panoptes.terrint.com](https://panoptes.terrint.com/#join).

- Membership is granted by the maintainers and can be declined or withdrawn.
- Members may receive triage or write access to the Panoptes repository.
- **Becoming part of the Panoptes project does not make you part of Terrint Creations.** It doesn't
  create employment, a partnership, ownership or any right to speak for Terrint Creations.
- Membership is unpaid and voluntary, like the project itself.

## Forks

Panoptes is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later. Anyone may fork it and develop it into
whatever they want, without asking, as long as they follow the license. Forks are independent
projects and should use their own name if they are distributed publicly, so users can tell them
apart from the main project.

## Free forever

Panoptes will remain free and open source. There will be no paid edition, no closed-source core and
no feature held back behind a payment.
